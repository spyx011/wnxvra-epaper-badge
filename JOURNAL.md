## 1. Project Overview

A custom ESP32-S3 badge featuring a 2.9-inch e-paper display, buttons, USB-C connectivity and a rechargeable battery.

## 2. Hardware Specifications

- **MCU:** ESP32-S3-MINI-1-N8
- **Display:** GDEY029T94, 2.9-inch e-paper
- **Resolution:** 296 × 128
- **Battery:** 3.7V 500mAh LiPo
- **Charging IC:** MCP73831T-2ATI/OT
- **Voltage Regulator:** AP2112K-3.3TRG1
- **USB-C:** TYPE-C-31-M-12
- **Buttons:** 5 × PTS810
- **PCB Size:** 169 × 66 mm
- **PCB:** 2-layer, 1.6mm, ENIG
